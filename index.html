2D Snake Game

Eat apples, grow your snake, and beat your high score!

Rules and controls

  • Use the arrow keys to steer the snake.
  • Each apple adds 1 point and grows your snake by 1 segment.
  • You start with 3 lives.
  • Hit a wall: lose 1 life and freeze in place.
  • If you have lives left, turn 90 degrees away to move again.
  • After a wall hit, you flicker with 3 seconds of protection from further wall damage.
  • Touch your own body: the game ends immediately.
  • Lose all your lives: the game ends.

Start will become available as we build the game.
