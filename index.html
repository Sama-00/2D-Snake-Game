2D Snake Game

Eat apples, grow your snake, and beat your high score!

Your browser must support canvas to play this game.

Press Start Game, then use the arrow keys.

Rules and controls

  • Use the arrow keys to steer. You cannot reverse straight into your body.
  • Each apple adds 1 point and grows your snake by 1 segment.
  • You start with 3 hearts. Hit a wall: lose 1 heart and freeze in place.
  • Turn 90 degrees toward an open square to move again.
  • After losing a heart, you flicker with 3 seconds of protection from more wall damage.
  • Touch your own body or lose all hearts: Game Over.
  • Press Space or Pause to pause. Use Restart Game after Game Over.

The best score is saved in this browser when local storage is available.
